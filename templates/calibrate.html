Checkerboard target

Recommended: print a 10 × 7 square board (9 × 6 inner corners), squares about 25 mm, on flat matte paper. Count inner corners, not squares.

Focus test

Place the checkerboard at least 1 foot (30 cm) away, fill the frame, and hold it completely still (a stand helps). Turn the lens focus ring until the sharpness score maximizes, then lock the ring.

Capture coverage
